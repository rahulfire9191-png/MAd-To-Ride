Our Story

More Than Just a Riding Club

Mad To Ride isn't just a group — it's a movement. From the winding ghats of Western Maharashtra to highway blasts at dawn, MTR riders share a bond forged on asphalt.

We welcome all bike lovers: from 150cc commuters to 1000cc superbikes. What unites us isn't the machine — it's the madness.
